# Mahmoud Awaleh

### Digital Architect · Governance Systems · GovTech · Institutional Design

I design and build digital systems that turn institutional knowledge, public services, and complex ideas into usable, trustworthy infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of governance, product strategy, communications, software, data, and institutional design. I am based in Hargeisa, Republic of Somaliland, and work independently while developing selected products and initiatives through M2 Creative & Consulting.

## Focus

- **Digital public infrastructure** — institutional records, public-service workflows, trusted information and operational systems.
- **Product and systems design** — translating complex requirements into usable, documented digital products.
- **Quality engineering** — UX/UI review, frontend and backend QA, data quality, security checks and release evidence.
- **Research and institutional strategy** — evidence-led analysis, standards, governance and implementation planning.

Project status is stated carefully: a live demo does not by itself establish production readiness, adoption, certification or institutional deployment. Concepts, prototypes, research and production systems are distinguished in project documentation.

## Principles

- Evidence before claims.
- Useful systems over decorative demos.
- Clear architecture, reproducible setup and maintainable code.
- Privacy, security, accessibility and data integrity by design.
- Accurate representation of the Republic of Somaliland and its institutions.
- Open-source reuse with respect for licenses and attribution.
